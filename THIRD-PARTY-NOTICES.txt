CROWNY adapted RainEffect integration

Codrops RainEffect, Lucas Bebber, 2015
https://github.com/codrops/RainEffect
Original repository statement: Integrate or build upon it for free in your personal or commercial projects. Don't republish, redistribute or sell "as-is".
https://tympanus.net/codrops/licensing/
This app adapts raindrops.js, create-canvas.js, random.js, times.js, water.frag and drop-alpha/drop-color maps. Original demo photos and layouts are not included.
Changes: transparent output, manual render lifecycle, input-excluded local screen texture, CROWNY touch controller and motion, drop shape transforms, canvas fallback, weather preview.
Weather API is not connected. Future adapter: window.CrownyWeather.set('rain'|'mist'|'clear'|'off').
